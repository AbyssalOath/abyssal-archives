Cloudflare R2

Optional. Needs an R2 subscription on your Cloudflare account. Steps (details in docs/DOWNLOADS.md):

  1. R2: create a bucket (e.g. abyssal-downloads).
  2. The bucket's Settings, Custom Domains: connect a hostname of your zone, e.g. dl.example.com (not the r2.dev URL: it is rate-limited and for development only).
  3. R2, Manage API tokens: create a token with Object Read & Write for that bucket only; copy its Access Key ID and Secret Access Key.
  4. Fill in the form, Test, then turn on uploads; once the sync shows your files, turn on serving.
